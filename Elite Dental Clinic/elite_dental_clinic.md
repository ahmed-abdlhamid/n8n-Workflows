## General Dental Information
This section provides foundational knowledge about oral health for AI agent responses and patient education.

### Basic Anatomy of Teeth
- **Enamel:** Hard outer layer protecting the tooth.  
- **Dentin:** Layer beneath enamel, sensitive to stimuli.  
- **Pulp:** Central part containing nerves and blood vessels.  
- **Gums (Periodontium):** Supportive tissues holding teeth in place.  
- **Types of Teeth:** Incisors, Canines, Premolars, Molars.

### Common Dental Issues
1. **Cavities (Caries):** Caused by bacteria and plaque. Prevent with brushing and flossing.  
2. **Gingivitis:** Early gum disease with inflammation and bleeding. Reversible with good hygiene.  
3. **Periodontitis:** Advanced gum disease leading to bone loss.  
4. **Tooth Sensitivity:** Often due to enamel wear or gum recession.  
5. **Bad Breath (Halitosis):** Can result from poor hygiene, diet, or medical conditions.  
6. **Tooth Erosion:** From acidic foods/drinks.  
7. **Bruxism (Teeth Grinding):** Often stress-related, damages teeth.

### Preventive Tips
- Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste.  
- Floss daily.  
- Use mouthwash.  
- Visit dentist every 6 months.  
- Limit sugary and acidic foods.  
- Wear mouthguards for sports.  
- Quit smoking/tobacco use.

### Oral Health and Overall Health
- Poor oral health linked to heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory issues.  
- Pregnancy: Hormonal changes increase gum disease risk.  
- Children: Early habits prevent lifelong problems.
