```
# Dental Materials and Clinical Usage Rules
```

- `## Restorative Materials (الحشوات مواد)` 

- `**Composite Resin (Filtek Z250 / Spectrum):**` 

- `Clinical Use: Used for D2330 (Anterior) and D2391 (Posterior) light-cured restorations.` 

- `Verification Rule: If the doctor notes "Composite used", the code MUST NOT be Amalgam (D2140).` 

- `**Glass Ionomer Cement (Ketac Molar / Fuji IX):**` 

- `Clinical Use: Used as a base/liner under composite or for primary tooth restorations.` 

- `## Endodontic & Anesthesia Materials (والبنج العصب مواد)` 

- `**Gutta-Percha Points:**` 

- `Clinical Use: Used for permanent obturation in Root Canal Treatments (D3310, D3330).` 

- `Audit Rule: If Gutta-percha is missing from the materials list in RCT, flag the claim as "Incomplete Documentation".` 

- `**Articaine 4% / Mepivacaine 3% (Local Anesthesia):**` 

- `Clinical Use: Essential for any invasive procedure (Extraction D7140, Endodontics D3330).`
