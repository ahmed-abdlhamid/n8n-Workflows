```
## Category: Dental Caries (الأسنانتسوس)
* **K02.3**: Arrested dental caries (متوقفأسنانتسوس)
```

- `Description: Tooth decay that has stopped progressing but requires monitoring or cosmetic restoration.` 

- `**K02.51**: Dental caries on pit and fissure surface, limited to enamel ( تسوس المينا)` 

- `Description: Early-stage decay confined to the outer enamel layer of pits and fissures.` 

- `**K02.61**: Dental caries on smooth surface, limited to dentin (العاج تسوس) - Description: Advanced decay that has penetrated into the dentin layer, often causing sensitivity. ## Category: Periapical and Pulpal Diseases (السن وجذر عصب أمراض) * **K04.01**: Reversible pulpitis (لرتجاعي العصب التهاب)` 

- `Description: Mild inflammation of the pulp; tooth can be saved without root` 

- `canal if treated early (e.g., direct/indirect pulp capping). * **K04.02**: Irreversible pulpitis (لرتجاعي غير العصب التهاب)` 

- `Description: Severe inflammation/death of the pulp; requires Root Canal Treatment (RCT) or extraction.` 

- `**K04.7**: Periapical abscess without sinus (ناسور بدون الجذر تحت خراج)` 

- `Description: Localized infection at the root tip causing severe pain and swelling.` 

- `## Category: Periodontal Diseases (والبرودونشيوم اللثة أمراض) * **K05.10**: Chronic gingivitis, plaque-induced (عن الناتجة المزمن اللثة التهاب الجير)` 

- `Description: Inflammation of gums without bone loss; requires scaling and polishing.` 

- `**K05.30**: Chronic periodontitis (المزمن السن دواعم التهاب)` 

- `Description: Deep infection causing destruction of bone support; requires deep scaling or periodontal surgery.`
